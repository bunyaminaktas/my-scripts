1. Extend the LVM partition to fill the disk:
    parted /dev/sda
    (parted) print
    (parted) resizepart 3 100%
    (parted) quit
    partprobe /dev/sda


2. Extend the physical volume (PV):
    pvresize /dev/sda3


3. Verify available free space in the volume group:
    vgdisplay


4. Extend the logical volume for root:
    lvextend -r -l +100%FREE /dev/mapper/ol-root
    // The -r flag automatically runs xfs_growfs.
